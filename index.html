I’m David Crespo Sánchez, 21, from Madrid. I’m studying a bilingual Double Degree in Business Intelligence and Business Administration at Universidad CEU San Pablo, currently abroad at The Hang Seng University of Hong Kong.

A previous exchange at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam—and 31 countries visited—has made unfamiliar environments feel like an invitation, not an obstacle. I’m analytical, curious and ambitious: drawn to new challenges, and especially to understanding why things happen, not just what the result is.

I’m interested in Data Analytics, Product Analytics, Advanced Analytics and Business Intelligence—combining technical analysis with real business problems to support data-driven decisions.

FRIENDS · CULTURE · HISTORY

Seoul

A trip with Erasmus friends

Travelling with friends made discovering Korean culture feel all the richer. Korean food was a highlight, and visits to major temples offered a very different pace. At the DMZ, learning more about the history between North and South Korea gave the trip another perspective.

The mix of time with friends and the history at the border is what I remember most.

FAMILY · A FIRST LOOK AT ASIA

Hong Kong

Just before my exchange began

I travelled with my parents and younger brother just before starting my exchange in Hong Kong. It was their first time in Asia, and seeing them enjoy the food, culture and energy of the city made it special. We visited Victoria Peak, the Avenue of Stars, temples, Big Buddha and Central.

It was a family trip, but it also marked the start of my own life in Hong Kong.

LANDSCAPE · NATURE

Tromsø

A different kind of place

The snow, the landscape and the atmosphere made Tromsø feel completely different from my city trips. Seeing reindeer and the northern lights made it one of my most special travel experiences—not for a checklist, but for the feeling of being somewhere so unlike home.

The snow and the landscape gave this trip a feeling unlike my city breaks.
